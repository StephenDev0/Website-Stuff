Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This privacy policy covers the StikDebug website at stikdebug.xyz. The short version: it doesn't collect your data.

The short version: This website has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. It's a static site that simply serves pages to your browser. The only data handling comes from our hosting and security providers, described below.

Information we collect

None. This website does not collect, store, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts to create, no forms to submit, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set any cookies of its own and uses no advertising or analytics cookies. Our security provider, Cloudflare, may set a strictly necessary cookie (such as __cf_bm or cf_clearance) to protect the site from abuse and verify that you are a real visitor. These are used only for security and are not used to track you.

The only thing the site itself stores is your light or dark theme preference, which is kept locally in your browser using localStorage. That preference stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Hosting and delivery

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which acts as a content delivery network and security proxy. As with any web server, these providers may automatically process standard technical information (such as IP addresses and browser type) to serve the site and protect it from abuse.

That data is handled by those providers, not by us, under GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Third-party links

This site relies on or links to third-party services such as GitHub, Cloudflare, and Discord. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for the practices of services we do not operate.

Open source

This website is open source. You can review exactly what it does by reading the code on GitHub.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach out on the Discord or open an issue on GitHub.
