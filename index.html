Privacy

Local-only by default.

StikDebug keeps your debugging workflow local, so your files and device details stay on your device.

No analytics

No background tracking or usage analytics are collected.

No accounts

StikDebug does not require sign-in or a hosted user profile.

On-device processing

Pairing files, scripts, and app state stay on your device.
